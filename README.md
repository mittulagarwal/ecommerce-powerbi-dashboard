# E-Commerce Sales Dashboard — Power BI

An interactive 2-page Power BI dashboard analysing global e-commerce sales data. Built with custom DAX measures, interactive slicers, and drill-through navigation for performance tracking across categories, regions, and payment methods.

## Business Problem

Identify which product categories, regions, and payment methods drive the most revenue for a global e-commerce business — and surface any underperforming segments that need attention.

## Tools Used

Power BI Desktop · DAX · Power Query · Interactive Slicers

## Key Business Insights

| Insight | Metric |
|---------|--------|
| Electronics | Highest revenue category by significant margin |
| Credit Card | 63.5% of all transactions |
| Asia | Leads in units sold across all regions |
| Revenue peak | January and March 2024 |
| Revenue dip | May–July 2024 — seasonal signal |

## Business Recommendations

1. **Increase Electronics inventory** — highest revenue driver, ensure stock availability
2. **Optimise Credit Card payment flow** — dominant payment method, reduce friction
3. **Expand Asia market** — highest volume region with room for growth
4. **Investigate May–July dip** — possible seasonal or competitive dynamic
